# Founding meeting

## Context

Monkeypuzzle has one user: its founder. The immediate uncertainty is whether a
second developer can install it, understand it, and find it useful in real work.
The PaaS should remain a hypothesis until CLI use and hosted pain are observed.

## Decisions

- Form Monkey Marketing as an evidence-driven growth function with two meetings
  per week.
- Define the north star as external repeat users, not stars or signups.
- Repair Homebrew distribution before design-partner outreach.
- Recruit the parallel senior IC persona first.
- Let one repeat user unlock hosted discovery, while requiring stronger evidence
  before a real PaaS build.
