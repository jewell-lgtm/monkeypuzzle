# Interview Guide

Ask about a recent real event before showing Monkeypuzzle.

## Screening

1. Walk me through the changes you had in flight last week.
2. How did you keep their branches, directories, terminals, and tools separate?
3. Show me the commands or folder layout if you can.
4. Which changes depended on other unmerged work?
5. What was annoying, risky, or slow?
6. What have you already tried?

## After first use

1. What were you trying to ship?
2. Where did you hesitate or need help?
3. What did Monkeypuzzle replace in your old workflow?
4. Which part felt valuable enough to repeat?
5. What would make you remove it tomorrow?

## Hosted-pain probe

Ask only after useful CLI behavior has been observed:

- What became harder across machines or collaborators?
- Who else needed visibility, and what decision were they trying to make?
- Where does that information live today?
- Would another local integration solve it?

Do not ask “Would you use a dashboard?” or pitch the PaaS before understanding
the event and current workaround.
