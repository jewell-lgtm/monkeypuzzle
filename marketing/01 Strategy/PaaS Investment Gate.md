# PaaS Investment Gate

The PaaS is a hypothesis about coordination and visibility, not the default next
phase of the CLI.

## Gate 1 — unlock hosted discovery

Begin focused PaaS discovery only after one [[00 Department/Department Brief#Definition of a real user|real user]] exists.

This permits interviews, a clickable prototype, and a deliberately manual
concierge test. It does not permit a production build-out.

## Gate 2 — justify a narrow hosted pilot

All must be true:

- three external repeat users exist;
- at least three observed workflows encounter the same cross-machine, team, or
  forge-visibility problem;
- the problem cannot be solved cleanly in the local CLI or an existing tool;
- two users agree to pilot a concrete hosted solution using real work;
- there is a credible privacy and trust model for repository metadata;
- the proposed service strengthens, rather than blurs, the workflow-manager boundary.

## Gate 3 — build for real

Require retained pilot usage over four weeks, repeated use of the hosted
capability, and evidence that at least one user would pay or stake an equivalent
organizational commitment.

## Decision rule

If the evidence points to local reliability, documentation, or integrations as
the bottleneck, invest there. Do not interpret lack of CLI adoption as demand
for a larger product.
