# Operating Cadence

Run two 30-minute meetings each week.

## Meeting A — pipeline and execution

1. Read [[02 Growth/Scorecard]].
2. Move every active prospect to a clear next action in [[02 Growth/Prospects]].
3. Inspect the live experiment and remove its biggest blocker.
4. Commit to at most three actions with owner and date.

## Meeting B — learning and decisions

1. Review new observations and quotes in [[03 Evidence/Signal Log]].
2. Decide whether the current bet is supported, contradicted, or still unknown.
3. Update messaging, personas, or product questions only when evidence warrants it.
4. Apply [[01 Strategy/PaaS Investment Gate]] if hosted-product work is proposed.

## Meeting output

Create `04 Meetings/YYYY-MM-DD — Pipeline.md` or
`04 Meetings/YYYY-MM-DD — Learning.md` from [[05 Templates/Meeting]]. Record:

- scorecard changes;
- decisions and their evidence;
- up to three dated commitments;
- unresolved questions;
- which notes were updated.

Alternate meeting types unless an urgent learning event makes another sequence
more useful.
