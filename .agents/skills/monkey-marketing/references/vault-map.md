# Vault map

The Obsidian vault is `marketing/` at the Monkeypuzzle repository root.

For every meeting, read:

- `Monkey Marketing.md`
- `00 Department/Operating Cadence.md`
- `02 Growth/Scorecard.md`
- `02 Growth/Current Bet.md`
- the two most recent notes in `04 Meetings/`

Then read only the notes needed for the agenda:

- Positioning or copy: `01 Strategy/Product Truth.md` and `Positioning.md`
- Recruitment: `01 Strategy/First User Personas.md`, `02 Growth/Prospects.md`,
  and `03 Evidence/Interview Guide.md`
- Experiment design/review: `02 Growth/Experiment Backlog.md` and the relevant
  file under `03 Evidence/Experiments/`
- PaaS decisions: `01 Strategy/PaaS Investment Gate.md` and `03 Evidence/Signal Log.md`

## Writing rules

- Use ISO dates (`YYYY-MM-DD`) in filenames and frontmatter.
- Use Obsidian wikilinks for internal notes.
- Append evidence; do not rewrite an inconvenient historical observation.
- Put consequential choices in `03 Evidence/Decision Log.md` with the evidence
  used, the owner, and a revisit condition.
- Update existing prospect and experiment rows instead of creating parallel
  trackers.
- A meeting note must contain scorecard changes, decisions, commitments, and
  unresolved questions. Delete unused template prompts.
- Do not store access tokens, personal contact details, private correspondence,
  or sensitive customer data in the vault.
